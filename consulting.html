Building Consultancy

Get expert advice before you build, buy or renovate. We help you plan properly, control costs and make sure the work on site matches what you paid for.

Our Services

Practical advice at every stage of a building project.

Pre-construction Advice

Feasibility, site assessment, budgeting and planning guidance before you commit money to a build.

Plans & Design Review

We review architectural and structural drawings, check them against your brief and budget, and flag problems early.

Cost Estimates & Bills of Quantities

Clear quantities and cost estimates so you can compare contractor quotes fairly and avoid surprises.

Site Supervision

Regular site visits and progress reports to check quality of work, materials used and progress against plan.

Building Inspection

Assessment of existing or newly built properties, with a written report on defects and recommended repairs.

Materials Advice

Guidance on the right materials for your project and budget, with supply through our building materials unit.

How We Work

A simple, transparent consultancy process.

You always know what we are looking at, what we found and what we recommend. Advice is given in writing so you can act on it with confidence.

  • Step 1: Consultation to understand your project, budget and timeline
  • Step 2: Site visit and review of plans or existing structure
  • Step 3: Written report, estimate or recommendations
  • Step 4: Supervision and follow-up visits where required
  • Step 5: Final review before handover or payment milestones
