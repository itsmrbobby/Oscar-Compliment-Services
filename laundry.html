How It Works

Simple from drop-off to collection.

Every item is checked and tagged when it arrives, so nothing gets mixed up and any concerns are raised before cleaning starts.

  • Step 1: Drop off your items, or ask us about collection
  • Step 2: Items are checked, counted and tagged
  • Step 3: Cleaned using the right method for each fabric
  • Step 4: Pressed, finished and neatly packaged
  • Step 5: Collected by you, or delivered where available
