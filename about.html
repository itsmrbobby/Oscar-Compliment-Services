About Oscar Complement Services

A diversified group built on distribution excellence, engineering precision, and a relentless commitment to quality across every industry we touch.

Our Story

From distribution roots to a multi-industry powerhouse.

Oscar Complement Services began as a focused distribution company, supplying quality goods to businesses and households across Nigeria. Over time, our deep understanding of supply chains, logistics, and customer needs led us to expand into engineering, construction, real estate, and specialized product lines.

Today, we operate six distinct business units — each run by specialists, unified by a single standard: reliability at scale. Whether you need a truckload of cement, a water purification system, a plot of land, or a cold-chain delivery of frozen poultry, Oscar Compliment Services delivers.
