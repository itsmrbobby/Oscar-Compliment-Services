CATEGORY PICTURES  (one photo per category tile on the Products page)

Put each photo in the folder shown, with the EXACT filename, as a .jpg.
Until a photo exists, the tile shows a clean icon, so you can add them one at a time.

Specs:  landscape 4:3, 800 x 600 px, JPG, under ~150 KB. One clear subject, bright and uncluttered.
Tip:    use your own product photos (or properly licensed ones). Don't use images copied from other websites.
New category added in the price sheet?  Use  <unit>/<category-name-lowercase-with-dashes>.jpg  ('&' becomes 'and').
