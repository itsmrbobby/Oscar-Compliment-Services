PRODUCT PHOTOS  (each product card on the Products page)

Name each photo after the product's SKU from the price sheet, as a .jpg, and put it in this folder:
    FF001.jpg  (Titus, frozen)      BM010.jpg  (Iron rod 10mm)      WP003.jpg      GS012.jpg

Specs: square 1:1, 600 x 600 px, JPG, under ~100 KB. Plain or lightly styled background, one product, well lit.
These are SEPARATE from the category pictures (images/categories/). A category photo shows the whole range;
a product photo shows that one item. Until a photo exists the card shows tinted artwork in its category's colours.
Add them gradually, there is no need to do all 345 at once. Start with your bestsellers.
