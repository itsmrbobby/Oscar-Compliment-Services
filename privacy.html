Privacy Policy

How we handle the personal information you share with us. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Who we are

Osca Complement Services (“we”, “us”) is based at Sebele Hotel bus stop, Eledi Atala, Itele Rd, Oke Oke, Ota 121101, Ogun State, Nigeria. You can reach us on 0810 580 3774 or info@oscarcomplementservices.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form, the quote form, email, phone or WhatsApp:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your company name, location and budget, if you provide them
  • The products or services you are asking about, including items you add to your quote list
  • Any message or specifications you write

The quote list is stored in your own browser so it stays with you between pages. It is only sent to us if you submit a quote request.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes and proposals, arrange deliveries or site visits, and keep a record of our dealings with you. We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing messages you have not asked for.

Who else may handle it

  • Form service. Messages sent through our forms may pass through a third-party form-handling service before reaching our inbox.
  • Google services. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, shows an embedded Google Map on the contact page, and may read our price list from a Google Sheet. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address when these load.
  • WhatsApp. If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to that conversation.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits for security and operation.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and quote records only for as long as we need them to serve you, meet legal or accounting obligations, or resolve disputes.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent you have given. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
